Consent and Choice: Experimental Evidence on User Behavior at Consent Banners

How do people respond to cookie consent banners? Using a browser extension that varies how easy it is to accept, reject, or customize cookies, we observe consent decisions during participants' everyday browsing. Many users close banners without making any choice, which makes website defaults critical, and our estimates suggest consumer surplus is highest when banners present choices clearly.

Cristiana Firullo*, Francesco Decarolis, Cristobal Cheyre, Alessandro Acquisti
