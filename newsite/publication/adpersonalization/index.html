Advertising, Personalization and Search Costs: Evidence from an Online Field Experiment

Does online advertising help consumers find what they are looking for, or distract them? In an online field experiment that randomly assigns participants to different advertising conditions, we test whether personalized ads lower consumers' search costs—helping them reach their goals in fewer steps and less time—beyond the effect of ad exposure alone.

Cristiana Firullo*, Cristobal Cheyre, Alessandro Acquisti
